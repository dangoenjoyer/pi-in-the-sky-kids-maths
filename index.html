How lessons work

Everything happens online, so all you need is a computer or tablet and a quiet spot.

  1. Book a time

    Choose a slot that suits your family and tell me what your child is working on.

  2. Click your link

    You will get a Zoom or Teams link by email, whichever you prefer. No downloads needed if you use a browser.

  3. Learn and practise

    Lessons use a shared whiteboard. Afterwards, parents get a short summary and a few practice questions.

Years 1 to 4

Lessons follow what your child is learning at school, plus a bit of stretch or catch-up where needed.

Years 1 and 2

Counting, place value, adding and subtracting, shapes and simple measuring.

Year 3

Times tables, bigger numbers, simple fractions, time and money.

Year 4

Multiplication and division, fractions and decimals, area and word problems.
